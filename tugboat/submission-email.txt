Some notes on typesetting:

- Please typeset it with luatex:  luatex soojin-nam-mf-gweb.tex
  It gives no overfull or underfull boxes.
- The few Korean words use UnBatang.ttf from TeX Live (unfonts-core),
  loaded by file name, not by a system font lookup.
- Figures B and C are drawn with MetaPost during typesetting, using
  luamplib, as in the translation itself. Figure A is a PDF.
- Figure A takes a whole page, so I put it at the end. Please move it
  wherever it fits best.
